What's included

Most limited companies take the whole set. Sole traders and landlords take the parts that apply. Either way, one fee and one point of contact.

  • Year-end accounts

    Statutory accounts prepared and filed at Companies House, with a plain-English walkthrough of what the numbers actually say about the year.

  • Corporation tax

    CT600 prepared and filed, with reliefs and allowances checked rather than assumed, and the payment date flagged in advance.

  • Self assessment

    Director and personal returns, including dividends, property income and anything else that needs declaring.

  • VAT

    Returns prepared and submitted under Making Tax Digital, with a look at whether you're on the right scheme rather than just the one you started on.

  • Payroll and pensions

    Monthly payroll, RTI submissions, payslips and auto-enrolment. Directors' salaries set at the level that actually makes sense for you.

  • Bookkeeping and cloud setup

    Either we do the bookkeeping or we get your system working properly so you can. Xero, QuickBooks or whatever you already run.

  • Advice in between

    Questions during the year don't generate an invoice. We raise issues that need attention before they become problems, rather than waiting to be asked.

How the fee works

You get a single monthly figure covering everything in the agreed scope. It's set from the actual shape of your business — turnover, transaction volume, payroll headcount, VAT scheme — not a package tier you have to squeeze into.

Phone calls and emails are part of the fee. If something genuinely falls outside the scope, such as an HMRC enquiry or a one-off restructuring piece, we quote it separately before we start rather than adding it to a bill afterwards.

Changing accountants takes one email from you

You don't have to tell your current accountant anything. That's our job, and it's a routine professional courtesy between firms.

  1. A short call

    Twenty minutes on the business, what you pay now, and what isn't working.

  2. A written quote

    Fixed monthly fee, scope set out clearly, no obligation to accept it.

  3. We write to your current accountant

    Professional clearance and the transfer of records happen between the two firms.

  4. We register as your agent

    HMRC correspondence and filing deadlines transfer across. You stop being the one chasing.
