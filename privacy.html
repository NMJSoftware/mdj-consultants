Privacy policy

How we collect, use and look after personal information, and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

MDJ Consultants Ltd is the controller of the personal information described here. We are a company registered in England and Wales, company number 15110513, at Canal View, 8B Slate Lane, Audenshaw, Manchester M34 5GW.

For anything about your personal information, email info@mdjconsultantsltd.co.uk, call 07713 697615 or write to the address above.

What we collect

When you get in touch

If you email or call us, we collect your name, email address, telephone number, and whatever you choose to tell us about your business or situation.

The enquiry forms on this website don't send anything themselves. They write your enquiry into an email in your own email app, and it only reaches us if you send it. Nothing you type into a form is stored by the website.

When you become a client

To act for you we collect the information needed to do the work and meet our legal duties. That can include your date of birth, address, National Insurance number, Unique Taxpayer Reference, residency details, financial and business records, and correspondence with HMRC and Companies House.

Before we can act, anti-money-laundering law requires us to verify your identity. We collect a copy of photographic ID and a proof of address for this.

Some of the information you give us about an HMRC enquiry may relate to suspected or alleged offences. We handle it only as far as needed to advise and represent you, and with extra care.

The tax estimator

The personal tax estimator runs entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us or stored anywhere.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • Replying to an enquiry and preparing a quote. This is a step you have asked us to take before entering into a contract, or our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Providing accountancy, tax and advisory services. This is necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • Verifying identity and keeping records. This is a legal obligation under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds Regulations 2017 and tax law.
  • Running the practice, such as handling complaints and dealing with our insurers and professional advisers. This is our legitimate interest in running the business properly.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only where we need to:

  • HMRC, Companies House and other authorities, when we act for you or the law requires it.
  • Your previous or next accountant, when you change adviser and ask us to transfer your records.
  • Service providers who handle information for us, such as our email and IT providers, cloud accounting software and identity-checking services. They act only on our instructions.
  • Our professional indemnity insurers, legal advisers and anti-money-laundering supervisor, where needed.

Some of these providers may store information outside the UK. Where they do, we make sure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as UK adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to an engagement: up to 12 months after our last contact.
  • Client files and working papers: normally six years after the end of the engagement or the tax year they relate to, longer if a claim or HMRC enquiry is still open.
  • Identity verification records: five years after our business relationship ends, as the Money Laundering Regulations require.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete information, where we no longer need to keep it
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it
  • ask us to transfer information you gave us to another organisation

Some rights are limited where we have to keep information by law, for example identity records. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We'll respond within one month.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics tracking.

Keeping information secure

We use access controls, encrypted storage and secure file transfer to protect personal information. Please don't send identity documents as ordinary email attachments; see Resources for how to send them securely.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if how we use personal information changes. Last updated 15 November 2026.
