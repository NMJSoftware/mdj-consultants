The founder

Neil Jones CAT

Neil started in an accountancy practice at fifteen, became a director and shareholder just after turning thirty, and left at forty as one of six partners in a firm of around sixty staff. Within it he was the technical lead, the knowledge resource, the staff liaison and the person problems were escalated to.

MDJ Consultants was founded on a straightforward premise: business owners get better outcomes when they can speak directly to someone with that depth of experience, rather than being routed through a call centre to a junior.

That is still how the practice works. When it matters, you get an adviser who leads from the front.

What that means in practice

Compliance handled quietly and on time. Specialist work — HMRC enquiries, forensic analysis, restructuring — handled by someone who has done it before rather than learning on your file.
