What this estimate covers

It applies the rates and allowances for the year you choose, in the order HMRC applies them: earnings and rent first, then savings, then dividends, then capital gains.

  • Personal allowance, including the taper above £100,000, and marriage allowance
  • Rental profit, with 20% relief for mortgage interest
  • Starting rate for savings, personal savings allowance and dividend allowance
  • Capital gains tax at 18% and 24% after the £3,000 annual exempt amount
  • Trading losses brought forward, and this year's loss set against other income
  • High Income Child Benefit Charge
  • Class 1 employee NI, Class 4 and voluntary Class 2
  • Student loan and postgraduate loan repayments
  • Basic rate band extended for pension contributions and Gift Aid

What it leaves out

Scottish income tax, Business Asset Disposal Relief and other capital gains reliefs, property losses, carrying trading losses back, and other reliefs. Employee NI and student loan repayments through payroll are worked out each pay period, so the actual figures can differ slightly from this annual estimate.
